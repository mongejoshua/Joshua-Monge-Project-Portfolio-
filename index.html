Energy Harvesting Vehicle Suspension

What?

  • Develop a suspension test rig simulating vertical vehicle motion.
  • Harvest dissipated energy using an electromagnetic generator.
  • Modular prototype developed within a $750 budget.

How?

Fusion 360 CNC Machining Additive Manufacturing FEA MATLAB/Simulink
  • Modeled assembly and components in Fusion 360.
  • Performed static stress analysis under suspension loading.
  • Tested assembly under cyclic loading.

Results?

  • Generated 1.3V from motion.
  • 3.5s settling time & 5.3% overshoot.
  • Measured 1.64 Hz natural frequency (vs 1.65 Hz theoretical).
  • Integrated mechanical, electrical, and control subsystems.

Jet Engine CAD Modeling and Assembly

What?

  • Design and assemble a detailed 3D jet engine model in SOLIDWORKS.
  • Recreate major engine components and demonstrate mechanical integration and rotational functionality.

How?

SOLIDWORKS Parametric Modeling Assembly Mates Mechanical Design
  • Individually modeled turbine blades, rotor assemblies, exhaust sections, outer casing, and mechanical fasteners.
  • Applied parametric modeling techniques to create complex geometries and mechanical interfaces.
  • Used assembly mates to constrain components, establish alignment, and enable rotational movement.

Results?

  • Completed a detailed jet engine CAD assembly with rotational functionality.
  • Demonstrated mechanical component integration and assembly alignment.
  • Developed SOLIDWORKS skills in part modeling, assembly design, and mechanical motion.

Truss Bridge Structural Analysis

What?

  • Design and fabricate a lightweight truss bridge.
  • Maximize load-to-weight ratio.
  • Predict internal member loading and structural failure.

How?

SOLIDWORKS Method of Sections Static Equilibrium FEA
  • Applied static equilibrium to calculate tensile/compressive forces.
  • Performed FEA to identify critical members.
  • Fabricated and experimentally load-tested final structure.

Results?

  • Achieved 457% structural efficiency.
  • Supported a load of 4.8 kg.
  • Successfully matched physical load-test results with predicted behavior.

Lightweight Generative Design (Coat Hanger)

What?

  • Redesign a coat hanger using aluminum.
  • Reduce weight and material while maintaining structural strength.

How?

Fusion 360 Generative Design Force Transmission Analysis
  • Analyzed potential failure locations and load paths.
  • Applied Generative Design to create lightweight geometries.

Results?

  • Reduced mass by 47%.
  • Improved durability with a Factor of Safety (FOS) of 4.306.

Bracket Generative Optimization

What?

  • Optimize a structural bracket maintaining a Factor of Safety (FOS) of 3.
  • Reduce unnecessary material.

How?

FEA Structural Optimization Static Stress Analysis
  • Defined loading conditions and preserved regions.
  • Produced lightweight alternative geometries via Fusion 360.

Results?

  • Bracket exceeded target with FOS of 3.211.
  • Reduced overall mass by 43%.
